Choose your pace

By 11:00
Confirmed

Breakfast and checkout

Breakfast is included at BBQ Lodge.

Option A
Relaxed seaside

San Fruttuoso and Santa Margherita Ligure

Take a morning ferry from Camogli to San Fruttuoso for the abbey and a swim. Have lunch by the water, then continue by boat to Santa Margherita for the promenade, gelato, and an unhurried evening. Stay in Santa Margherita or return to Camogli.

San Fruttuoso map Santa Margherita map

Best for: swimming, seafood, minimal logistics, and a slow Riviera day.

Option B
Active cycling

Valtellina e-bike ride toward Tirano

Leave Camogli early and drive to Sondrio or Tirano. Rent e-bikes for a section of the Sentiero Valtellina cycle path, passing vineyards, villages, and mountain scenery. After the ride, either stay in the valley or continue toward Bergamo for Tuesday's work day.

Driving route Cycle route map

Trade-off: roughly 2.5-3 hours of driving from Camogli before the ride. Check e-bike availability and plan a rapid charging stop for the rental car.

Mountain alternative: if cycling weather is poor, take the Bernina Railway from Tirano instead. Do not plan both the bike ride and the railway on the same day.

Know before you go

Logistics

01

Camogli access

BBQ Lodge is reached on foot by a downhill path with steps. Pack a light overnight bag instead of carrying all luggage from the car.

02

Weekend parking

San Rocco parking costs approximately €15 on Saturday and Sunday. The restricted area is closed to parking from Friday 19:00 until Monday 06:00.

03

Europcar requirements

Bring the main driver's physical licence, ID, flight ticket, eVoucher, and a physical named payment card with PIN. The €500 deposit is blocked on credit cards or charged to debit cards.

04

Return-time conflict

Both the Europcar return and Wizz Air departure are booked for 08:00 on June 24. Contact Europcar before travel to arrange an earlier or after-hours return.
